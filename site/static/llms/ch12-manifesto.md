## Key concepts

- **The manifesto is real**: every quote in the Holy Book has a verifiable source. This chapter attributes them. No invented citations.
- **Hacker ideological lineage**: LulzSec → Zone-H → IRC/EFnet → YTCracker → Yung Innanet → the underlying movement. Not random aesthetic choices — a traceable genealogy.
- **"Villain but not Monster"** ethic: a villain breaks systems to reveal them; a monster harms people for pleasure. The distinction is morally non-negotiable in the scene.
- **FP academic lineage**: Church → Moggi → Wadler → Wlaschin → Ocarina. The ROP implementation is grounded in formal mathematics, not invented.
- **AI as RL**: the analogy to Reinforcement Learning explains why Ocarina's strict typing and ISTQB vocabulary make AI co-authorship effective. The constraints are the reward signal.
- **SaaS refusal** has an economic argument: the SaaS industry's ARR fraud and LP/VC extraction model is documented. Ocarina's MIT + gift model is a political stance against this.
- **Survivor psyche**: the author's cohort experienced the 2000s underground internet (harassment, state-level threats in teenagers' bedrooms). The psychological adaptations (radical self-reliance, extreme diligence, distrust of institutions) are features of the code, not bugs.
- **DEF CON** is the annual tribal validation venue. YTCracker's trajectory (NASA hack at 17 → DEF CON performer → corporate cybersec consulting) embodies "live as a white hat, or die as a black hat".
