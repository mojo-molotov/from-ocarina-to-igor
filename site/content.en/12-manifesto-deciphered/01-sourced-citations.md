## Why sources matter

The Holy Book _never cites_ its sources. Not by negligence, by **scene convention**: if you recognize, you belong; if you don't, you read the _surface_ text, which also works. It's a literary device as old as _underground_ communities. LulzSec did it on their pastebins.

Restoring sources doesn't betray the spirit: it **makes it readable** for those who didn't grow up on those IRC channels. And the target user (a developer or tester discovering Ocarina) has no reason to have grown up there. Without this map, half the reading is missing.
