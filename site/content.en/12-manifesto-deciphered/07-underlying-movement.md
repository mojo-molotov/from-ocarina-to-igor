# 12.07&nbsp;—&nbsp;The underlying movement

> Three possible trajectories for anyone in the 1999-2012 hacker scene: (1) stay _black hat_ and end up in prison (at best), (2) drop off entirely, (3) **rebuild oneself alone through Research**. The Holy Book speaks of the third. That's where Ocarina comes from.

## 1. The key passage from the Holy Book

> _From Hell to where we are today._  
> _Saved by Research, by good values, by thankless work. Not the kind that makes you shine. The kind passed down by those who know how to detect a potential and save it before it's too late._
>
> _**NO ONE saved us, we saved OURSELVES.**_  
> _**Thanks to what SCIENCE says.**_
>
> _We could have been thirsting for blood, instead we became thirsting for skills. We are people who dedicated their lives to these screens, to this science, while you harassed teenagers in Dota chats or did who-knows-what, always showing off, always proving you're the prettiest, strongest, smartest, most dominant._
>
> _**We may be retards, but we'll stay online until the end!**_
>
> _**We are a real FAMILY!**_  
> _**And we NEVER sleep!**_

It's the Holy Book's most _autobiographical_ passage. It defines a category: "_those who left the black hat scene without selling out, without cynically pivoting, but by \_converting_ to scientific research\_".

## 2. Trajectory

### Phase 1&nbsp;—&nbsp;Hell (≈ 1998-2013)

| Characteristic | Detail                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Place          | EFnet IRC, BBS, Warez scenes, Zone-H defaces                                                            |
| Activities     | _Grey_ to _black_: carding, defaces, under-the-counter exploits                                         |
| Social status  | Marginal, sometimes criminally compromised                                                              |
| Culture        | _2600_, _Phrack_, _zines_, hacktivism                                                                   |
| Traumas        | Online violence, _doxing_, OPSEC paranoia, very sudden losses of friends, hard drugs, sometimes arrests |

"_Hell_" refers less to criminality than to **the social and psychological environment**: isolation, paranoia, permanent violence, profound loneliness, loss of loved ones. Many did prison time. Even more dropped off. A few took their own lives (see [`06-yung-innanet-vxug.md`](06-yung-innanet-vxug.md)).

### Phase 2&nbsp;—&nbsp;the conversion

The turning point for the minority that gets out "_through the top_":

- **Discovery of _real_ science**: algebra, type theory, formal logic, λ-calculus.
- **Reading the Researchers rather than tech magazines**: Knuth rather than _Wired_, Pierce rather than JavaScript tutorials.
- **Self-taught academia**: often without an official degree. Papers, MOOCs, online communities, conferences.
- **Reformulating the ethic**: from "_hack for fun_" to "_truly understand to truly build_".

A **conversion without rupture**: you don't disavow the previous years, you change _use_ of the same skills.

### Phase 3&nbsp;—&nbsp;the thankless work

> _Saved by Research, by good values, by thankless work. Not the kind that makes you shine. The kind passed down by those who know how to detect a potential and save it before it's too late._

The **"_thankless work_"**: extremely clean code, strict types, endless refactors, rigorous tests, documentation almost no one will read, hours of near-monastic isolation. **That's Ocarina.** It's the opposite of _hype_, of being _disruptive_, of the _10X engineer_.

It's also **under-radar mentoring**: "_those who know how to detect a potential and save it before it's too late_" suggests an informal transmission chain, outside institutions, where people who made it _pull_ others toward them.

## 3. "_NO ONE saved us, we saved OURSELVES_"

- **No institutional academic mentor** (HEC, Polytechnique, MIT). Self-taught.
- **No social reintegration program**. No psy.
- **No enlightened family support** (often: families that didn't understand the context or just didn't know).
- **No employer who "_spots the talent_" and takes the years needed to train it**&nbsp;—&nbsp;the author positioned himself after self-training.

What's _NOT_ being claimed:

- _Total independence_. The next sentence ("_those who know how to detect a potential and save it_") suggests there were **elders** within the scene itself.
- _Contempt for education_. _Research_ is explicitly the savior.

It's a **"_alone with others like me_"**, not solipsism. It's the _crew_ from [`04-zoneh-irc-efnet.md`](04-zoneh-irc-efnet.md) maturing with its members.

## 4. "_We NEVER sleep_"

Ritual phrase of the hacker scene since _the 1980s BBSes_. Appears in:

- _2600_ e-zines.
- _Hacktivist_ scene slogans ("_the internet doesn't sleep, neither do we_").
- 2000s cyber-criminal aesthetics.

The Holy Book picks it up as-is. **Ethic of permanent work**, refusal of the work/life split, identity fused with the technique.

## 5. "_We are retards_"

Ironic inversion of an accusation. The word "_retard_" was used against this scene (by teachers, parents, _normies_, other engineers) for years, in the sense of: _socially handicapped, asocial, marginal, autistic_.

The author picks it up **as a banner**&nbsp;—&nbsp;**identity reclamation**, exactly like _Nerd_ is reclaimed by _Nerdcore_ (see [`03-ytcracker-nerdcore-digital-gangster.md`](03-ytcracker-nerdcore-digital-gangster.md)).

> "_We may be retards, but we'll stay online until the end!_"

"_You called us retards; we accepted it, it doesn't faze us; but look where we are \_now_, and where you are.\_"

## 6. "_FAMILY_"

> _**We are a real FAMILY!**_

A virtual and organic family, well beyond Ocarina. The family that made Ocarina possible.

Belonging criteria:

- Having lived through the 1999-2012 scene (or having studied its history with respect).
- Having converted that experience into real academic / scientific skills.
- Refusing the _big-talk_, refusing _hype_, refusing to _sell bullshit_.
- Holding to an ethic of _thankless work_.

## 7. Those who "_harass on Dota_"

> _while you harassed teenagers in Dota chats or did who-knows-what, always showing off_

Dota 2 is sadly famous for this, like many MOBAs.

Here, the author points to **the other category**.  
Those who had the same sociological base (cheap "_geeks_" in high school, ending up on IRC or Dota) but **channeled their energy elsewhere**:

- Online harassment, _doxing_ for fun, building hunt lists, claiming to harass kids, the disabled, the vulnerable.
- _Cyberbullying_, _swatting_.
- Online political _envenoming_ (_alt-right_ recruited very effectively in this demographic).
- First-degree _crypto bros_ (not scientific crypto, _cash grabs_).
- Low-tier influencers (cf. what _Tugan Bara_ finally denounced fervently during the Covid crisis in the French-speaking scene&nbsp;—&nbsp;[`10-infopreneurs-tugan-bara-ai.md`](10-infopreneurs-tugan-bara-ai.md)).

The Holy Book splits the scene in two: **the _makers_** and **the _destroyers_** (who are _parasites_). This split isn't neutral&nbsp;—&nbsp;it _holds_ historically. The same generation produced YTCracker (Bryce Case Jr., now a cybersec consultant at huge companies) **and** professional doxxers.

These are two distinct trajectories that split into two tribes, as implicitly described in Kent Beck's _Extreme Programming Explained: Embrace Change (XP)_: those who spend their lives stabbing each other in the back, and those who have a sense of community and mutual aid.

## 8. Lineage with Ocarina

| "_Builder_" trajectory               | Manifestation in Ocarina                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Conversion to formal science         | Strict types, λ-calculus, PEP 695                                                                  |
| Thankless work                       | A single runtime dep, extremely thorough documentation, dense internal tests                       |
| Ethic of transmission                | Holy Book free, MIT, exposure to LLMs                                                              |
| Refusal to sell bullshit             | Refusal to be another pytest plugin, refusal of `async`, refusal of SaaS, **back to fundamentals** |
| Family                               | "_It's my car_" + invitation to _real enthusiasts_                                                 |
| "_Bug that can't be killed_" posture | Ocarina is small, copyable, autonomous, public, free, uprootable                                   |

Ocarina **is** the software projection of this trajectory. Its _design_ betrays its **history**, evidences a **heritage**.

## 9. Deep respect for the end user

A developer adopting Ocarina without knowing this context will likely adopt **a subset of the ethic**:

- He'll _scaffold_ his first project extremely fast.
- He'll use ROP because it's elegant.
- He'll like the _mypy strict_ rigor.
- He'll appreciate auditability.
- He has the whole ecosystem of _skills_ and contents indexed directly for LLMs as well as for humans, to be fully independent.
- He has what it takes to fully adapt his tool to his own expertise by extending it with AI rather than fighting with "_geek tricks_".

It's a _transmission_ without pain.

With this context, the scream becomes **readable**: it's the voice of someone who survived, who saw too many friends not survive (see [`06-yung-innanet-vxug.md`](06-yung-innanet-vxug.md)), and who can no longer stand _slipologues_ telling him how to code while they know nothing and only produce "_garbage_". The scream is an **act of identity reclamation** that many of his contemporaries failed to make.
