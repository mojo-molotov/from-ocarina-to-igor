# 12.10&nbsp;—&nbsp;Infopreneurs, Tugan Bara, AI

> The ecosystem _opposite_ to Ocarina. Not another testing framework: another _cultural model_. We have to name what the Holy Book fights to understand _why_ it fights. And why AI is killing this model.

## 1. What is "_infopreneurship_"

### Definition

**Infopreneur** = entrepreneur whose product is _sold information_: online courses, coaching programs, e-books, masterminds, "_premium newsletter_" subscriptions. The product isn't physical.

Typical business model:

```
1. Free: YouTube, podcast, call-to-action newsletter
   ↓ (authority building, lead capture)
2. Front-end product: €27 e-book / €97 mini-course
   ↓ (trust building, upsell)
3. Back-end product: flagship course at €997 / €2497 / €4997
   ↓ (client qualification)
4. Continuum / coaching: mastermind €12,000/year, 1:1 support at €5,000/day
```

The commercial mechanism is called a **funnel**, propped up by _copywriting_&nbsp;—&nbsp;the art of writing persuasive _sales texts_.

### American origin

Tradition born in the United States in the 1970s-1980s with:

- **Dan Kennedy**&nbsp;—&nbsp;_No B.S._ series. Classic _direct response marketing_.
- **Jay Abraham**&nbsp;—&nbsp;_Getting Everything You Can Out of All You've Got_.
- **Frank Kern**, **Russell Brunson** (ClickFunnels), **Eben Pagan**&nbsp;—&nbsp;the 2000s _digital marketing_ generation.

Scheme: _problem → agitation → solution → CTA + urgency + scarcity_.  
Stacked bonuses. Fake urgency. Decreasing price (_value stacking_). 30-day "_satisfied or full refund_" guarantee.

### Arrival in France

The model came to France via a handful of actors who _Frenchified_ American copywriting.  
Among them: **Tugan Bara**.

## 3. Why this ecosystem is unhealthy

1. **Sells dreams.** The promise isn't "_you'll learn X_", it's "_you'll earn €Y/month_". Yet Y depends on _thousands_ of variables the course doesn't control.

2. **Survives by recruitment, not by value.** Once a program is bought, the _real_ product is the next one. And the next. And the next.

3. **Recruits vulnerable audiences.** Typical buyers: burned-out salaried workers, young parents in transition, expats losing their bearings. They _need to believe_, so they _believe_.

4. **Standardizes a model that only works for a few.** Success stories exist, but they're survivorship bias. Most buyers never make more than €15 of "_accidental_" revenue.

5. **Creates fake community.** Masterminds, private exchange platforms, bootcamps _look like_ community but are _an extension of the product_. Leaving the program = losing the "_community_". Some even formalize cultic drifts and call it an "_infopreneur community_".

6. **Cannibalizes real skills.** At least Tugan's courses had real value, but that's frankly not the case for most. For all that time wasted on "_garbage courses_", you get a young person who's just been dumbed down further.

7. **Spreads a counter-ethic of _sell at all costs_.** _Sales ethics_ (the real kind) are explicitly presented as a handicap in _underground_ programs. People learn to "_hack_" the prospect's psychology. For every one person who really understands the strings, a thousand will simply commit offenses.

## 4. Why AI kills this model

### The infopreneur offer's core = copywriting and synthesizing recycled resources

The _product_ sold isn't _knowledge_&nbsp;—&nbsp;knowledge is often available for free (on YouTube, or in €20 books).

The _product_ is:

- **Packaging** (creating an _offer_).
- **Orchestration** (funnel that leads from free to paid, endless _upsells_).
- **Storytelling** (the authority built via free content, notably via _clipping_: infopreneurs are all "_friends_" and participate in many "_interviews_" then rebroadcast everywhere by _affiliates_; it's often a _decoy_ to amass _distribution power_. Precisely what Tugan Bara denounced about _Cluely_ and others).

### AI ejects precisely these skills

| Skill                             | Before AI                                 | With AI (2024+)                                 |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 10-page sales text                | Days of work + a senior copywriter at €5k | A few hours with Claude/GPT, 80% senior quality |
| 12-email funnel                   | Weeks + ConvertKit/ActiveCampaign tool    | At most a few days with a custom GPT            |
| Authority storytelling            | Years of YouTube + production             | Months with AI scripts                          |
| Landing-page refresh (sales page) | Designer + front dev + copywriter         | A few prompts + Lovable / v0 / Cursor           |

- The _cost_ of a funnel collapses
- The _volume_ of funnels flooding the market explodes
- The _signal_ (quality emerging from noise) drops
- Buyers become **immune** to generic, hollow _copywriting_ without real **identity** ("_yet another funnel_")

### The AI Slop Effect on infopreneurship

As all actors use the same AI tools to generate the same trashy texts, markets _desensitize_. That's the documented _AI slop effect_. The press, marketing emails, LinkedIn ads: everything becomes conformist, codified, in a sense. What remains is what's _truly exceptional_, and it's neither on LinkedIn nor in funnels powered by ad networks overrun with professional bullshitters.

### Tugan Bara understands before the others

His 2023-2024 trajectory:

1. **Co-founds Tugan.ai**&nbsp;—&nbsp;he _becomes_ the tool that automates his core trade.
2. **Sells Tugan.ai in 2024**&nbsp;—&nbsp;he exits _in time_.
3. **Repositions as Arnaud Labossière**, further from the initial infopreneur model, while staying true to himself.

This _confirms_ the Holy Book's diagnosis: the intelligent actors in this ecosystem _leave it_.
