## 2. VX Underground

### Definition

**VX Underground** is an organization and website dedicated to **archiving malware samples** and **distributing _underground_ literature** on the topic. URL: [`vx-underground.org`](https://vx-underground.org)

| Field             | Value                                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Founded           | ~2019                                          |
| Declared founder  | **smelly_vx** (Twitter `@vxunderground`)       |
| Original sysadmin | **kayos / Yung Innanet**                       |
| Current status    | Largest public malware repository in the world |

Source: [vx-underground&nbsp;—&nbsp;Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vx-underground)

### Importance

VX Underground filled a void:

- Old malware-archive sites (vxheaven, etc.) had closed or lost activity.
- Cybersec researchers needed publicly accessible samples for analysis.
- No reliable academic centralization.

VX Underground became, in a few years (2019-2025):

- **The** reference for malware researchers.
- An influential Twitter account (real-time commentary on major attacks).
- A historical literature repository (hacker zines, hard-to-find academic papers).

### kayos / Yung Innanet's role

He was the original sysadmin who set up the infrastructure. Hosting, DNS, _torrents_, distribution. _Vital_ aspect of such a structure (technical moderation of malicious content is non-trivial: we're literally talking about _hosting viruses_).

He was also one of the _technical voices_ of the VXUG community on Twitter, contributing to the culture around the project.

## 4. The quote picked up by Ocarina

In its current version, the Holy Book **directly quotes the lyrics** of `true colors` (in English, caps preserved):

> _**THE FUCK YOU THINK THIS IS?**_  
> _**You HOLLOW!**_  
> _**YOU HOLLOW, YOU UNDERSTAND ME?**_  
> _**YOU'RE WORTHLESS, YOU'RE FUCKING TRASH!**_
>
> <p align="right"><i>(btw: RIP, DG descendant…)</i></p>

The link on the block points to the track: [SoundCloud&nbsp;—&nbsp;_queed-inc/true-colors_](https://soundcloud.com/queed-inc/true-colors)  
It's the cultural bridge connecting the manifesto's quotes (Lulzboat, Cryptoilluminati, YTCracker, Yung Innanet): **all belong to the same DG lineage.**

### The French pivot

The raw English block is followed, in the Holy Book, by a **French block** centered and written in caps&nbsp;—&nbsp;the _translation-extension_ that prolongs the invective by addressing the _slipologue_ by name:

> _ON VA TE REMPLACER ET TU RETOURNERAS HARCELER TES PAIRS SUR DOTA ET TE BRANLER SUR DES MACROS VBA COMME UNE MERDE!_  
> _PUTAIN D'INCOMPÉTENT QUI NOUS EMMERDE!_  
> _PUTAIN DE PARASITE!_  
> _PUTAIN D'INCAPABLE!_  
> **_YOU FUCKED WITH US!_**

The last line&nbsp;—&nbsp;`YOU FUCKED WITH US!` in italic English&nbsp;—&nbsp;closes the sequence with a direct echo of the **`Don't fuck with us`** mantra from the carding/defacement scene (YogyaCarderLink, see [`05-indonesian-hackers.md`](05-indonesian-hackers.md)). The shift from **future conditional** _(don't…)_ to **past** _(you fucked…)_ is the rhetorical pivot: _we warned you, you did it anyway, now own up_.

Again, references to this mantra also appear in another YTCracker track, _we are vulnerable_:

> Can you fuck with us?  
> Can you deploy toys rubber duck with us?  
> Can you destroy noise on a NUC with us?  
> More importantly, can you make a buck with us? (hmm)

[`we are vulnerable`, YTCracker, 2025](https://soundcloud.com/ytcracker/ytcracker-we-are-vulnerable)

### Analysis

| Layer                          | Text                                                                | Origin                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Raw English, scream         | `THE FUCK YOU THINK THIS IS? / You HOLLOW! / …`                     | `true colors` lyrics, Yung Innanet                               |
| 2. Signature                   | `(btw: RIP, DG descendant…)`                                        | Tribute                                                          |
| 3. French, addressed invective | `ON VA TE REMPLACER ET TU RETOURNERAS HARCELER TES PAIRS SUR DOTA…` | Francophone war cry (probably from the _BreachForums_ community) |
| 4. Final English refrain       | `YOU FUCKED WITH US!`                                               | Adapted YCL mantra (future → past)                               |

The Holy Book's author didn't _translate_&nbsp;—&nbsp;he **borrowed Yung Innanet's voice** for the raw cry, then continued in French to address it personally to his target enemy (the _slipologue_ DOTA + VBA). It's a four-tiered literary construction, each tier coming from a distinct scene (digicore / DG / IRC pamphlet / Indonesian carding).

## 5. Why this quote, in this spot

The quote falls in the Holy Book's "_First feedbacks_" chapter, right after a buildup of invective against the "_slipologues_" who want to dictate to the author what he should do:

1. _Setup_: the author lists the counter-proposals received ("_rewrite in Rust for performance_", "_drop typing_", etc.).
2. _Climax_: the `true colors` passage, a verbal explosion borrowed from Yung Innanet.
3. _Signature_: `(btw: RIP, DG descendant…)`, the author _signs_ his borrowing&nbsp;—&nbsp;he isn't stealing.
4. _Pivot_: French extension addressed to "_ON VA TE REMPLACER ET TU RETOURNERAS HARCELER TES PAIRS SUR DOTA…_" → AI will replace these _slipologues_, exactly as the author demonstrates with `ocarina-with-ai-example`.
5. _Refrain_: `YOU FUCKED WITH US!`, YogyaCarderLink echo.

The quote's _tone_ is essential: it's the **owned rage** of a creator who was told for years he was on the wrong path. Not gratuitous rage: a _cathartic_ rage that closes a long patience _setup_ documented throughout the chapter. Probably something like 20 years of anger, 20 years of being called "_autistic_" and staying silent.

## 6. Selective references

Ocarina's author chose this quote knowing who Yung Innanet was. A **coded tribute**:

- To a respected technical sysadmin.
- To a rage / digicore musician, who was _talented and beloved_.
- To a person who _built the infrastructure_ of an important cultural project (VXUG).
- To a tragic life.

That this passage is the Holy Book's rhetorical _climax_ is no accident. It's the passage where the author goes farthest in the invective, borrowing the voice of someone who can no longer speak and whose death rattled the entire community.

It's a form of respect and transmission.
